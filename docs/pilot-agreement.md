# Victress Founding Design Partner Pilot Agreement

Effective on the date the team's representative accepts it by email or by signing below. Between Victress LLC ("Victress") and the team named below ("the Team").

**Team:** ____________________  **Representative (coach, captain or manager):** ____________________  **Email:** ____________________

## 1. The pilot
A free pilot of 4 to 6 weeks for one competitive team, starting on the date we both confirm by email. No payment is due from the Team, now or later, for the pilot. Nothing in this agreement is an offer of funding, coaching, mentorship, scholarships, sponsorship, employment or a placement, and Victress offers none of these.

## 2. What the Team does
- Uses Victress during the pilot and uploads practice files (demo or replay files) on a regular schedule, for the games Victress supports for uploads at that time.
- Takes a 15 minute feedback check in each week with Victress, led by the coach or captain.
- Agrees to a case study about what we learn, only if both the Team and Victress approve its final text in writing. Nothing is published without that approval.
- Makes sure every player whose name, voice or chat is in a file the Team uploads is a member of the Team, knows the file is being uploaded, and agrees to it. Players under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission.

## 3. What Victress does
- Gives the Team free, priority access to the features available during the pilot, including the Player Resume and, as it becomes available, Scrim Intelligence.
- Listens to the Team's feedback and gives the Team direct input into what Victress builds.
- Answers questions by email at hello@victressesports.com.

## 4. Data and privacy
- Files and information the Team uploads stay private to the Team's accounts. Victress uses them only to provide the pilot, to check that its features work correctly, and to improve them.
- Victress does not sell, publish or share the Team's files or any player's information. It shares information only with the service providers listed on the Privacy page, as needed to run the service.
- The Team can ask at any time for its files and its players' accounts to be deleted. Email hello@victressesports.com and Victress will delete them and confirm by email.
- The Terms and Privacy pages on victressesports.com apply to everyone who uses Victress during the pilot.

## 5. Ending the pilot
Either side can end the pilot at any time by email, for any reason, with no cost. When it ends, the Team's accounts stay on the free Rookie plan unless the Team asks for deletion. Sections 1 (no offer of funding or placement) and 4 (data and privacy) continue after the pilot ends.

## 6. Feedback and results
Feedback is welcome and is given freely. Victress may use it to improve its products without owing the Team anything for it. Victress does not promise any result from the pilot, such as a ranking, a performance gain or an opportunity.

## 7. Governing law
The laws of the State of Utah, United States, govern this agreement.
