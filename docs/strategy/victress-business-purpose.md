# VICTRESS

# Business Purpose, Creating Shared Value & Strategic Direction

**Turn practice into progress.**

## 1. Why Victress Exists

Victress exists to improve how competitive esports talent is developed, recognized, evaluated, and given opportunities to advance.

Competitive esports has enormous talent, ambition, and participation, but the infrastructure connecting players to meaningful development and competitive opportunities remains fragmented.

Players practice without always receiving meaningful performance feedback. Their competitive histories and achievements are scattered across platforms, screenshots, recordings, spreadsheets, and self-reported statistics. Teams and coaches often lack consistent ways to evaluate player development. Recruiters frequently rely on informal networks, subjective impressions, and incomplete information to identify talent.

As a result, players can struggle to demonstrate their abilities, understand how to improve, and gain access to opportunities that match their potential.

These problems affect the broader competitive ecosystem, but their consequences are not distributed equally. Women competitors face additional barriers involving visibility, harassment, informal gatekeeping, access to competitive networks, and recognition of their achievements.

Victress exists to help change this.

Our purpose is to build the infrastructure that makes competitive development more measurable, talent more visible, evaluation more transparent, and opportunities more accessible.

We believe competitive potential should not be limited by who a player knows, how visible they are, or whether they have access to established competitive networks. Players deserve meaningful ways to demonstrate what they can do and opportunities to turn their development into progress.

## 2. Creating Shared Value Is the Business Strategy

Victress is founded on the principle of Creating Shared Value, developed by Michael Porter and Mark Kramer.

Creating Shared Value means generating economic value by addressing real social and economic needs. It is not corporate philanthropy added to an otherwise unrelated business. It is not greenwashing, performative social responsibility, or a marketing campaign designed to improve public perception.

It means building the business around solving problems that matter to people and improving the systems in which the business operates.

For Victress, the connection is fundamental:

- Players need better tools to develop, demonstrate, and communicate their competitive abilities.
- Teams and coaches need more reliable information to understand performance and support development.
- Recruiters need better ways to discover and evaluate talent.
- Competitive communities and organizations need infrastructure that helps their players progress.
- Underserved competitors, including women in esports, need more equitable access to visibility, development, and opportunity.

These needs are connected. Addressing them creates value for players, organizations, and the competitive ecosystem while establishing a sustainable business for Victress.

The social value and the business value are not competing objectives. They are created through the same products, infrastructure, relationships, and improvements.

Victress succeeds commercially by making the competitive ecosystem work better.

## 3. How Victress Creates Shared Value

Victress applies the three dimensions of Creating Shared Value to the way it builds and operates its business.

### A. Reconceiving Products and Markets

Victress develops products that address unmet needs in competitive player development and recruitment.

Its platform is built around three connected capabilities:

**Scrim Intelligence**

Turn private competitive practice into meaningful performance information.

Players and teams should be able to understand performance beyond basic match results, identify strengths and weaknesses, evaluate trends, and make more informed development decisions.

Social value:

- Make meaningful performance feedback more accessible.
- Help players understand their development.
- Give competitors evidence of their abilities beyond subjective impressions.
- Improve access to analytical tools that may otherwise be unavailable to developing players and smaller organizations.

Business value:

- Establish a differentiated performance intelligence product.
- Create recurring subscription value.
- Increase platform engagement and retention.
- Build reliable competitive data that supports the wider Victress ecosystem.

**Player Resume**

Give every competitive player a credible way to represent their competitive identity, experience, achievements, and development.

A Player Resume should bring together verified performance, competitive history, game and role information, accomplishments, and relevant highlights.

Social value:

- Improve player visibility and recognition.
- Reduce dependence on fragmented records and informal referrals.
- Help players communicate their competitive experience.
- Give overlooked talent a more accessible way to demonstrate its abilities.

Business value:

- Establish a central player identity within Victress.
- Encourage player adoption and continued engagement.
- Create a shareable asset that connects players to the broader ecosystem.
- Supply structured information for team evaluation and recruitment.

**Scout Desk**

Connect competitive talent with teams, coaches, collegiate programs, and recruiters through structured discovery and evaluation.

Social value:

- Expand access to discovery and evaluation.
- Make talent easier to find beyond existing personal networks.
- Help organizations evaluate players through more consistent evidence.
- Improve the connection between player development and competitive opportunities.

Business value:

- Establish recruitment software as a commercial product.
- Generate organizational subscriptions and recurring revenue.
- Create valuable relationships with teams and institutions.
- Strengthen the network connecting players and organizations.

Together, these products address a connected problem: practice should lead to progress, progress should be demonstrable, and demonstrated ability should be easier to recognize and connect with opportunity.

### B. Redefining Productivity in the Value Chain

Victress must create shared value through the way its products and operations are designed, not only through the features it sells.

This includes:

**Data integrity and verification**

Establish reliable methods for collecting, parsing, validating, and presenting competitive data. Clearly distinguish verified demo-parsed information, API-derived information, and self-reported information.

Verification establishes the provenance and integrity of evidence. It does not automatically establish a player's overall ability, potential, or suitability for a team.

**Transparent evaluation**

Present performance in context. Metrics should help inform decisions rather than become simplistic judgments of player quality. Evaluation should recognize that competitive roles, team coordination, strategies, and circumstances influence individual statistics.

**Privacy, consent, and player control**

Give players meaningful control over their identities, competitive records, profile visibility, and contact information. Separate account identity, public player identity, verified records, and private contact details. Design privacy around actual disclosure risks.

**Accessible development infrastructure**

Build tools that are useful to players and teams at different stages of competitive development, rather than designing exclusively for established professional organizations.

**Trustworthy recruitment processes**

Help recruiters evaluate evidence consistently while preserving player agency. Contact information and private identity details should not be disclosed without appropriate consent.

Social value comes from more trustworthy, accessible, and transparent systems. Business value comes from better data, stronger user confidence, higher-quality products, and lasting relationships with players and organizations.

### C. Enabling Competitive Ecosystem Development

Victress recognizes that player development and recruitment do not happen in isolation.

A strong competitive ecosystem depends on relationships between players, teams, coaches, leagues, tournaments, collegiate programs, communities, and recruiters.

Victress aims to provide connective infrastructure that helps these groups work together.

This includes opportunities to:

- Connect amateur and collegiate competitive environments with broader talent discovery.
- Help leagues and communities document player and team performance.
- Give coaches and organizations better tools to support development.
- Create more consistent pathways between competitive participation and recruitment.
- Establish relationships with organizations that share an interest in developing competitive talent.

As these relationships grow, Victress can help strengthen the ecosystem while expanding the usefulness and reach of its platform.

The value is mutual: organizations gain useful infrastructure, players gain visibility and development opportunities, and Victress builds a stronger network of participants and customers.

## 4. Women in Esports: A Core Expression of Victress's Purpose

Victress is called Victress for a purpose.

The advancement of women in competitive esports is not an unrelated charitable activity, an occasional marketing campaign, or a separate business attached to the company.

It reflects a fundamental reason Victress exists: competitive talent deserves the opportunity to develop, be recognized, and advance.

Women competitors have faced barriers involving harassment, limited access to competitive networks, informal gatekeeping, visibility, and the difficulty of demonstrating their abilities to teams and recruiters.

Victress intends to address these barriers through the same infrastructure that defines its business.

**Visibility**

Player Resume helps women competitors document their experience, achievements, and verified competitive performance in a credible and accessible format.

**Development**

Scrim Intelligence can help women competitors access meaningful performance analysis, identify areas for improvement, and track their development through competitive practice.

**Opportunity**

Scout Desk can help make women competitors more discoverable to teams, coaches, collegiate programs, and recruiters through structured, evidence-based evaluation.

These are not three separate charitable programs. They are applications of Victress's core business capabilities to meaningful barriers within competitive esports.

The objective is not to create a separate standard of competition or isolate women into a separate product. It is to improve the infrastructure through which talent is documented, developed, evaluated, and connected to opportunity.

Women competitors should benefit directly from this commitment. The improvements to data integrity, privacy, development, and recruitment should also benefit the broader competitive ecosystem.

This is how Victress creates shared value: by addressing real barriers while improving the systems and markets in which its business operates.

## 5. A Business Model That Sustains the Purpose

Victress is a commercial technology business. Revenue is essential because it allows the company to develop, maintain, and expand the infrastructure required to fulfill its purpose.

Its business model is built around the value provided to different participants in the competitive ecosystem.

- Rookie: Free access that introduces players to the Victress platform.
- Pro: $7.99 per month or $75 annually, supporting individual player development and advanced platform capabilities.
- Team: $49 per month, supporting team-level performance and development workflows.
- Scout: $249 per month, providing recruitment and talent discovery capabilities for organizations.

These products and subscriptions are not separate from the purpose. They are the economic model through which Victress sustains and expands its infrastructure.

As the platform becomes more useful to players, teams, and recruiters, its commercial value can grow alongside its social value.

Victress should pursue sustainable growth without making unsupported promises about scholarships, placements, coaching, mentorship, or guaranteed recruitment outcomes.

The company's responsibility is to build reliable infrastructure that makes development and opportunity more accessible.

## 6. How Victress Will Build and Measure Its Impact

Victress will not measure its purpose through marketing impressions, promotional campaigns, or statements of support.

It will measure whether its products and operations create meaningful improvements.

**Player development**

- Access to and use of meaningful performance feedback.
- Demonstrable development over time.
- Improved ability to document competitive experience.
- Reduced administrative burden in tracking performance.

**Visibility and recognition**

- Growth in players with credible, verified competitive records.
- Ability to share and communicate competitive experience.
- Meaningful evaluations and engagement from teams and recruiters.

**Opportunity**

- Recruiter discovery and evaluation activity.
- Player-initiated and recruiter-initiated connections.
- Competitive opportunities facilitated through the platform.
- Documented transitions into teams, collegiate programs, and other competitive environments where measurable.

**Trust and integrity**

- Data accuracy and provenance.
- Effective consent and privacy controls.
- User confidence in how information is collected and shared.
- Safety reporting, response, and resolution effectiveness.

**Business sustainability**

- Player and team adoption.
- Product engagement and retention.
- Subscription conversion and recurring revenue.
- Organizational relationships and partner retention.
- Cost-effective expansion of the competitive ecosystem.

Both sides matter. Social impact without a sustainable operating model cannot support long-term progress. Commercial growth without meaningful value to the ecosystem would contradict the reason Victress exists.

## 7. Long-Term Strategic Direction

Victress intends to become an essential part of the infrastructure connecting competitive practice, player development, performance evidence, and recruitment.

The long-term opportunity is larger than any individual feature or game integration.

It is the opportunity to improve how competitive talent moves through the esports ecosystem:

**Practice → Performance Evidence → Development → Recognition → Opportunity**

Victress will build toward this through evidence-led development:

1. Understand the real barriers faced by players, teams, coaches, and recruiters.
2. Build reliable performance intelligence, player identity, data provenance, and privacy infrastructure.
3. Prove that the platform improves player development, visibility, and evaluation.
4. Strengthen relationships with communities, leagues, teams, and collegiate programs.
5. Expand recruitment capabilities and scale the business sustainably.

Progress will be determined by operational readiness, technical reliability, stakeholder needs, and measurable results—not arbitrary deadlines or unvalidated assumptions.

## 8. The Victress Commitment

Victress believes competitive potential deserves the opportunity to become competitive progress.

That requires more than encouraging players to work harder. It requires better infrastructure, more reliable evidence, meaningful development tools, transparent evaluation, and stronger connections to opportunity.

We are building that infrastructure because the esports ecosystem needs it, because players deserve it, and because addressing these needs creates a stronger and more sustainable competitive industry.

The advancement of women in esports is part of that purpose—not an accessory to it.

The opportunity to improve competitive development and access belongs to the entire ecosystem. Victress intends to help make that possible.
